## 五、FAQ（Chat 起草；只写通用、不承诺诊所政策的答案）
1. **How do I know if I should see a psychiatrist? · 我怎么知道自己是否需要看精神科？**
   EN: If low mood, anxiety, sleep or attention problems have lasted for weeks and are affecting your work, studies or relationships, it is worth speaking with a psychiatrist. You don't need to be sure — an assessment is how we find out together.
   ZH: 如果情绪低落、焦虑、睡眠或注意力问题持续了几周，并影响到工作、学习或人际关系，就值得找精神科医生谈谈。你不需要先确定答案，评估就是一起弄清楚的过程。
2. **Do I need a referral letter? · 需要转诊信吗？** → 留空（诊所政策，清单 2.5）
3. **What happens at the first visit? · 第一次看诊会做什么？**
   EN: Dr. Hanni will ask about your current concerns, your history and what you hope to get help with. If you have a list of current medications or past medical reports, feel free to bring them — but you can come without them.
   ZH: 涵倪医生会了解你目前的困扰、过往经历，以及你希望得到哪些帮助。如果有正在服用的药物清单或以往的医疗报告，可以带来；没有也没关系。
4. **Will I have to take medication? · 看精神科就一定要吃药吗？**
   EN: Not necessarily. Whether medication could help depends on your assessment, and the decision is made together with you.
   ZH: 不一定。是否需要药物取决于评估结果，并且由你和医生一起决定。
5. **How much does a consultation cost? · 看诊费用是多少？**
   EN: Please contact the clinic for current consultation fees.
   ZH: 最新的看诊费用请直接联系诊所。
6. **Can someone come with me? · 可以请人陪同吗？** → 留空（清单 4.5）
7. **Will my information be kept confidential? · 我的资料会保密吗？**
   EN: Yes. Your medical information is handled in line with medical confidentiality and Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act.
   ZH: 会。你的医疗资料按照医疗保密原则和马来西亚个人资料保护法处理。
8. **What if I need urgent help? · 如果我需要紧急帮助怎么办？**
