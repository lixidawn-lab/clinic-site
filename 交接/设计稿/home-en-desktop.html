Dr. Lau Harn Ni

Psychiatrist in Melaka · Doctor of Psychiatry (UKM)

I’m a psychiatrist who listens closely.

With over a decade of experience, I help patients through mental health challenges with holistic care that combines medication and psychotherapy.

Specialising in adolescent mental health, mindfulness-based therapy, dialectical behaviour therapy (DBT), cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT), trauma-focused CBT (TF-CBT), and stress and emotion management.

Here I share mental health insights and stories of compassionate care, and invite you to explore human connection and healing with me.

How we begin

  1. 01
    First consultation
    Reach out on WhatsApp or by phone to arrange a time that works for you.
  2. 02
    Understanding your concerns
    We listen to what you’ve been experiencing, how long it has lasted, and how it affects daily life.
  3. 03
    Professional assessment
    Dr. Hanni carries out a psychiatric assessment to understand what may be going on.
  4. 04
    Planning your care together
    We discuss the options and agree on next steps together, at a pace that feels right for you.
